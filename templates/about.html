What this app does

The app sends the submitted visitor attributes to the saved scikit-learn model and displays its predicted class and estimated probability.

Features used

  • Daily time spent on site
  • Age
  • Area income
  • Daily internet usage
  • Gender (encoded as a Male indicator)

Prediction interpretation

The probability shown is the model's estimated probability for the click class. It is not a guarantee of user behavior or a measure of model accuracy.
